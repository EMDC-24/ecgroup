How we work

Clear responsibility from start to finish.

The company best suited to the project leads it. Other EC Group companies join only when their expertise is needed.

Our approach

A simple way to work.

01

Understand the work

We start by understanding what needs to be done, who is involved, the main constraints and the expected result.

02

Choose the lead company

The EC Group company closest to the main work takes responsibility for the project.

03

Add other expertise where needed

Other EC Group companies join only when the project needs their expertise.

04

Keep delivery coordinated

When several companies are involved, we keep responsibilities, communication and delivery clear.

Working with partners

We work with clients and partners as one team.

Our projects often involve government agencies, consultants, contractors, technology companies and international partners. We define roles early and keep communication clear.

Consulting partners

Public institutions

Consulting, engineering and technology support for government programs and projects.

Delivery principles

Keep ownership clear. Keep the work practical.

The structure should make a project easier to deliver, not add another layer.

Clear ownership

Every part of the project has a named lead and clear responsibility.

Practical decisions

Advice, designs and technology choices are based on how they will work in practice.

Direct coordination

When several companies are involved, we coordinate dependencies directly instead of passing work between disconnected teams.

Ways to work with us

Joint EC Group team. Several EC Group companies work together on one project with clear roles.

With external partners. EC Group can join a wider consortium or delivery team with other firms.
