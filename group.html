About us

Four specialist companies under one parent company.

EC Group is the parent company of Euroconsult Pakistan, ECMDC, RMS Technologies and Atlas Intelligence. Together, the four companies work across engineering, management consulting, technology, data and AI.

Why EC Group

Large projects often need more than one kind of expertise.

An infrastructure project may also need software and data. A government program may need consulting, technology and implementation. An international partner may need local engineering, systems integration and project support.

EC Group makes it easier for our companies to work together on these projects. Each business keeps its own team and expertise, while the parent company provides direction and coordination when several companies are involved.

Clients can work with one company directly, or with more than one company through EC Group.

The role of EC Group

The parent company supports the businesses and brings them together when needed.

EC Group sets overall direction, supports shared business development and partnerships, and coordinates projects that involve more than one company.

Direction

We set group priorities and help each company grow in its own field.

Joint projects

When a project needs several areas of expertise, EC Group coordinates the companies involved.

Partnerships

We work with local and international partners on opportunities that span more than one business.

How the group works

Specialist companies, working together when it makes sense.

We do not involve every company in every project. The company with the right expertise leads.

If a project needs more than one area of expertise, the relevant companies work together with clear responsibilities.
